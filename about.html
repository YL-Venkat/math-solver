About Math Solver

A simple and interactive web platform for exploring mathematical and cryptographic tools.

What is Math Solver?

Math Solver is a web-based platform designed to make common mathematical and cryptographic operations quick and easy to perform.

The project brings frequently used mathematical tools into one place, allowing students, programmers, and mathematics enthusiasts to calculate and explore different concepts through a simple and interactive interface.

What Can You Do?

Mathematical Tools

Arithmetic operations, factorials, GCD, LCM, Euler's Totient, prime number tools and more.

Number Conversions

Convert between Binary, Decimal, Octal and Hexadecimal number systems.

Cryptographic Tools

Explore Caesar, Shift and Affine ciphers, modular exponentiation and Legendre symbols.

Interactive Experience

Get quick results through a clean interface with responsive design and smooth animations.

Our Goal

The goal of Math Solver is to bring useful mathematical and cryptographic tools together in one accessible platform while keeping the experience simple, fast and easy to use.

Whether you're solving a quick calculation, learning a mathematical concept, or experimenting with cryptographic operations, Math Solver aims to provide the tools you need in one place.
