About This Project

Math Solver is a clean, fast platform to explore math and crypto tools.

Whether you're a student, programmer, or just curious about numbers, our tools provide instant solutions for:

  • Arithmetic operations and factorials
  • GCD, LCM, and Euler’s Totient (ϕ)
  • Prime number detection and listing
  • Classic ciphers like Caesar, Shift, and Affine
  • Modular exponentiation and Legendre symbols
  • Base conversions (Binary, Decimal, Octal, Hex)

Results are instant — no long explanations, just clean outputs in a smooth, animated UI.

Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with responsive design and soft animations.

Thanks for visiting — explore, calculate, and enjoy!
